## 📱 Responsive Behavior

### Desktop (>768px)
- Split screen layout (50/50)
- Form on left, image on right
- Full image visibility

### Tablet (768px - 1024px)
- Split screen maintained
- Slightly adjusted proportions (60/40)
- Image may be cropped slightly

### Mobile (<768px)
- Single column layout
- Image hidden or shown as small header
- Focus on form content
- Better performance

## ✨ Design Features

### Common Elements Across All Images
1. **Brand Colors:** Consistent use of #667eea and #10b981
2. **Subtle Backgrounds:** Low opacity gradients (8-12%)
3. **Professional Figures:** Realistic medical professionals
4. **Medical Symbols:** Crosses, hearts, equipment
5. **Decorative Elements:** Floating dots and patterns
6. **SVG Format:** Scalable and lightweight

- **Doctor Consultation:** Clipboard, stethoscope, professional attire

- **Hospital Building:** Architecture, emergency entrance, ambulance
